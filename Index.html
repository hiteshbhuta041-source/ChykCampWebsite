Frequently asked questions

What is CHYK Camp?

CHYK Camp is an immersive retreat led by Pujya Swami Swaroopananda, the Global Head of Chinmaya Mission. It provides an opportunity to explore the divine knowledge found in Hindu scriptures, surrounded by like-minded individuals from across Australia and New Zealand.

At this National CHYK Camp, you will learn directly from Pujya Swamiji's new book, You Are the Sky: An Ancient Guide to Modern Feelings, and receive teachings from the author himself.

Through this topic, you will delve deeper into the emotions you experience in everyday life. Each chapter explores emotions such as anger, fear, jealousy, guilt, love and sadness, among many others. The text invites you to question whether you control these emotions or whether they control you.

These emotions can feel intense and overwhelming, sometimes blocking out our light. But we are not the storm; we are the sky. The book blends timeless Vedantic wisdom with modern psychology to offer a practical roadmap for spiritual seekers.

Who is Pujya Swami Swaroopananda?

Pujya Swamiji is the Head of Chinmaya Mission Worldwide. After studying under the pre-eminent Master of Vedanta, H.H. Swami Chinmayananda-ji, Pujya Swamiji has become a guiding light to youth all over the world.

With his charismatic yet tranquil nature, Pujya Swamiji has a rare voice that blends authenticity with accessibility, and logic with heart. He teaches the messages of the scriptures in a way that inspires and captivates all.

Where will I be staying?

We will be staying at the lovely Crosslands Youth & Convention Centre in Galston, New South Wales, Australia.

Crosslands Youth & Convention Centre is a family-friendly campsite located in Galston Gorge, approximately an hour's drive from Sydney's CBD. The main entrance is accessible via Crosslands Road by car, trailer, van or minibus.

How do I get to the campgrounds?

For those travelling from Sydney: Driving is the primary way to get to the campgrounds. We will also be organising carpooling for anyone who needs it.

For those travelling from outside Sydney: We will organise transport between the airport and the venue. A form to collect your flight details will be available soon, so keep an eye out for further information.

What's included in my ticket?

As a part of your ticket you'll be provided with accomodation, food, activity books merch, and the following in flight entertainment -

Lectures by Swami Swaroopananda: The Global Head of Chinmaya Mission will guide us through the intricacies of our emotions.

Hands-on discussion groups: Take part in insightful discussions with fellow learners, encouraging deeper realisations and meaningful connections.

Interactive and fun activities: Test your knowledge through engaging activities that help you put the teachings into practice.

These sessions will explore:

  • Experiencing, not suppressing: Acknowledging our emotions without allowing them to define us.
  • Responding vs. reacting: Finding the freedom to pause and choose how we respond to life's challenges.
  • Navigating modern digital pressures: Building inner awareness so that external validation does not dictate our self-worth.
  • Destigmatising vulnerability: Challenging the pressure on young people to have everything figured out.
  • Chapter-by-chapter practicality: Exploring each core emotion through in-depth philosophy and practical insights.
